About

Research that answers questions people are actually asking.

Over the past fifteen years I have led and contributed to research programmes spanning infectious disease surveillance, maternal and child health, and the design of resilient primary health care systems. My approach combines rigorous quantitative methods with close collaboration with the communities and institutions that use the findings.

I currently serve as Senior Lecturer at [University Name], where I teach epidemiology and research methods at postgraduate level and supervise doctoral candidates working on health systems and population health topics.

Beyond the university, I act as a technical adviser to public health agencies and development partners, supporting programme evaluation, data systems design and evidence-to-policy translation.

I am a strong advocate for open science and locally led research capacity — mentoring early-career researchers and building collaborations that outlast individual grants.
